# Google AdSense Approval Checklist (2026)

## Overview

If you want to get Google AdSense approval for your AI Blogging Website, follow this complete checklist before applying.

---

# 1. Essential Pages (Mandatory)

Your website must have:

- About Us
- Contact Us
- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- Disclaimer
- DMCA Policy (Recommended)

---

# 2. Quality Content Requirements

Before applying for AdSense:

- Publish at least 20-30 unique articles.
- Each article should contain 800-1500+ words.
- Review AI-generated content manually.
- Avoid plagiarism.
- Add relevant images.
- Use proper headings and formatting.

### Good Article Structure

- Introduction
- Key Benefits
- Features
- Pros & Cons
- Comparison
- Conclusion
- FAQ

---

# 3. Domain Requirements

Use a custom domain:

✅ Recommended

- yourwebsite.com
- yourwebsite.in
- yourwebsite.net

❌ Avoid

- yourwebsite.blogspot.com
- yourwebsite.github.io

---

# 4. Website Design Checklist

Ensure your website is:

- Mobile Responsive
- Fast Loading
- Easy to Navigate
- Free of Broken Links
- Search Enabled
- User Friendly

---

# 5. Traffic Requirements

Traffic is not mandatory for approval but recommended.

### Suggested Minimum

- 300-500 Monthly Visitors
- 20+ Indexed Pages
- Basic Organic Traffic

---

# 6. Google Tools Setup

Configure:

- Google Search Console
- Google Analytics 4
- Bing Webmaster Tools

Submit:

- sitemap.xml
- robots.txt

---

# 7. Recommended Content Niches

High CPC Categories:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Technology
- Software Testing
- Cyber Security
- Cloud Computing
- Programming
- Finance

Avoid:

- Copyrighted Content
- Auto-Generated Spam
- Adult Content
- Gambling Content

---

# 8. SEO Requirements

Every article should contain:

- SEO Title
- Meta Description
- SEO-Friendly URL Slug
- Featured Image
- H1, H2, H3 Headings
- Internal Links
- External References

### Example

Title:
Top AI Testing Tools in 2026

Slug:
top-ai-testing-tools-2026

Meta Description:
Discover the best AI-powered testing tools for modern QA teams in 2026.

---

# 9. Revenue Features

Recommended Features for Blogging Website

## AdSense Module

- Ad Placement Management
- Revenue Tracking
- CTR Monitoring
- RPM Analytics

## Affiliate Marketing

- Product Links
- SaaS Reviews
- Tool Recommendations

## Sponsored Content

- Sponsored Articles
- Product Reviews
- Featured Posts

---

# 10. Final AdSense Approval Checklist

Before applying, ensure:

- [ ] Custom Domain Connected
- [ ] SSL Enabled (HTTPS)
- [ ] 25+ Published Articles
- [ ] About Us Page
- [ ] Contact Us Page
- [ ] Privacy Policy Page
- [ ] Terms & Conditions Page
- [ ] Sitemap Available
- [ ] robots.txt Available
- [ ] Google Search Console Configured
- [ ] Mobile Responsive Design
- [ ] No Broken Links
- [ ] Fast Website Speed
- [ ] Indexed Content in Google
